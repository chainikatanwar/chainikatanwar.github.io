Professional impact

Platforms I designed and led at Agoda and Expedia, and the results they delivered. Details stay at a level that respects confidentiality.

  • EU DAC7 compliance across 27 markets

    Agoda

    A new EU law required Agoda to collect, verify and report identity and income data for every property host, by a fixed legal deadline, with no system in place. I owned it end to end: a seller data workflow in the partner portal, a PII-safe reporting pipeline, and automatic payout unblocking once hosts complied.

    $27Mestimated penalties avoided; about 97K properties brought into compliance

  • Central tax rules platform

    Agoda

    Tax exemptions were hard-coded in the search engine, owned by another team, with no audit trail. I designed an API-driven rules service with an embedded, cached library that added no search latency, and won over a reluctant Search team by building the design around their goals.

    10 days → minutesper tax rule change; 5 jurisdictions onboarded

  • Securing a shared invoice platform

    Agoda

    After wrong invoices went out and no caller could be traced, I added Open Policy Agent authorization to about 30 open endpoints and led a risk-ordered rollout, with a rollback flag and invoice-volume monitoring at every step.

    12 teamsonboarded with minimal downtime

  • AI-assisted service consolidation

    Agoda

    Seller data logic had ended up split across a .NET and a Kotlin service. Using Claude for cross-language migration and test generation, I consolidated it into one Kotlin service and moved traffic market by market with a 50/50 split.

    2 sprintsinstead of an estimated quarter; 30 markets migrated

  • Tax reporting rebuilt as an ETL

    Agoda

    New country tax reports needed custom code in a complex shared library. I led a proof of concept that used Claude to extract the legacy logic and rebuild reporting as a scheduled, test-covered ETL.

    ~2 daysper new report in the pilot, down from 1–2 weeks

  • Security tooling reliability

    Expedia

    Led a real-time monitoring and alerting system with an availability, quality and performance dashboard for 10+ security tools, and automated malicious URL blocking on Zscaler from approved Jira tickets.

    99.9%tool availability; 50% better threat-blocking efficiency

Commitment Loop for Fireflies.ai

Capstone: research to prototype

Problem
Meeting assistants capture action items well, but many are never resolved after the meeting ends.
Approach
16 interviews disproved my first hypothesis and showed the real barrier: follow-up depends on someone chasing. I prioritized with RICE, wrote PRDs and built a prototype.
Result
Honest status outcomes and private reminders, measured by a 14-day honest-resolution North Star with guardrails.
